About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Communication Systems at Chalmers University of Technology, Gothenburg, Sweden, working on physical-layer security for integrated sensing and communication.

My research applies detection, estimation, and optimization methods to develop secure and robust wireless systems, with a focus on integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and physical-layer security under jamming, spoofing, eavesdropping, and interference. Current research includes spoofing detection in cooperative multistatic ISAC, secure beamforming and sensing for monostatic NOMA–ISAC, RIS-assisted anti-jamming, and interference mitigation in multi-antenna and distributed wireless networks.
